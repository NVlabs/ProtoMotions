Unitree G1
==========

Select ``--robot-name g1`` for the 29-DOF G1 model. The default asset is
``mjcf/g1_holo_compat.xml``; do not assume another G1 asset has the same body
frames, hands or inertias merely because it has 29 actuators.

Default Contract
----------------

* Config: ``protomotions/robot_configs/g1.py`` (``G1RobotConfig``).
* Structure: 33 bodies, 29 joint DOFs; +X forward, Z up.
* Observation anchor: ``torso_link``; physical root: ``pelvis``.
* Target bodies: ``torso_link``, ``head``, ``right_ankle_roll_link``,
  ``left_ankle_roll_link``, ``left_wrist_yaw_link``, ``right_wrist_yaw_link``.
  The hands are tracked at the wrist link, a frame every G1 hand variant has;
  ``left_rubber_hand`` and ``right_rubber_hand`` remain in the asset as rigid
  children of the wrists.

The config declares a bent standing pose and per-actuator-family PD gains,
armatures, velocity limits and effort limits. Gain formulas use a 10 Hz natural
frequency and damping ratio of 2, with joint-specific multipliers. These are
simulation controller defaults, not a claim about the settings of every G1
hardware variant. Experiments define the action mapping separately.

Motion And Deployment
---------------------

Use G1-retargeted motion, not source human joint coordinates. Keep the
checkpoint's saved asset and sensor/observation contract when exporting or
switching simulators. Changing the selected hand body changes target geometry.

* :doc:`../getting_started/seed_g1_csv_preparation`: convert G1 CSV motion.
* :doc:`../tutorials/workflows/retargeting_pyroki`: retarget human motion to G1.
* :doc:`../tutorials/workflows/g1_deployment`: export and deployment workflow.
* :doc:`../getting_started/pretrained_models`: G1 policies and example motions.
